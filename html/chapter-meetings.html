    Vietnam Veterans of America Chapter 279 - Chapter Minutes

    Our VVA Chapter meetings are held the Second Wednesday of the month at 6:30 pm, at the Millard American Legion located at 13913 S Street - in Strip Mall - on East Side of the Road in Omaha, Nebraska. Phone (402) 895-1324

    All Vietnam Veterans and their spouses are invited to attend our meetings.
